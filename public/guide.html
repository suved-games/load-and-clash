How to play Hex Men

A duel for two players, or a battle between two teams. Build up points, unlock stronger attacks, and pick the right defense before the 5-second timer runs out.

Join a game

  1. Open the link your friend sent you. It works on a phone, tablet or laptop.
  2. No link? Open the game website, type the 5-letter room code into Room code, and tap Join room.
  3. Type your name and tap Join room. You land in the waiting room with the host.
  4. Tap Ready when you are set. Changed your mind? Tap Unready.
  5. The host taps Start match once you are ready, and the game begins for both of you.
  6. Keep the page open while you play. If the first page load takes up to a minute, the game server is waking up. Just wait.

If you are the host

  1. Type your name, choose 1v1 or Team vs Team, and tap Create room.
  2. Send the invite link (or the 5-letter room code) to your friends.
  3. In a 1v1 room, Start match switches on only when your friend has pressed Ready. For a team game, see Team vs Team below.

How a round works

  1. Both players pick a move in secret. You have 5 seconds.
  2. Tap Defend or Attack to open a wheel of moves, or tap Load. Tap a move to pick it, then tap Lock in.
  3. Shortcut: double-tap (or double-click) a move to lock it in at once.
  4. On a laptop, press the move's key and then Enter.
  5. If time runs out before you lock in, Load is played for you.
  6. Both moves are shown side by side with their pictures, the scores change, and the next round starts right away.

Your moves

  • Load adds 1 point to your score.
  • Defend with Mag, N.V, Shield or Rebound. You can use any defense at any time.
  • Attack once your score has reached the attack's number. Everyone starts at 0, so you have to Load first.

How you win

  • You attack and your opponent does not use the matching defense (or is loading): you win.
  • You attack and your opponent uses the matching defense: you lose points equal to the attack's number.
  • You are attacked and you use the matching defense: you are safe, and the attacker loses points.
  • You are attacked and you do not use the matching defense: you lose.

Special rules

  • Dam against Rebound: the player who used Dam loses.
  • Touch E against Rebound: the two scores are swapped.
  • Touch E against any other defense, or against Load: the Touch E player takes the other player's whole score.
  • Touch E against any other attack: the Touch E player loses.
  • Both players attack: the bigger attack wins the game. If both use the same attack, each of you loses that many points and the game goes on.

Team vs Team

Two teams of 2, 3 or 4 players take turns fighting 1v1 games. The first team to win enough games takes the match.

  1. The host picks Team vs Team, the team size (2v2, 3v3 or 4v4) and how many wins a team needs, from 7 to 25.
  2. Everyone who joins starts in Waiting. Tap a team's Join button to join Team Red or Team Blue. To switch teams, tap the other team's Join button if it has room, or go back to Waiting first.
  3. Player 1 of each team (the first player in its order) picks the team's name and one of 15 colors.
  4. Both teams must be full. Then everyone presses Ready.
  5. Each team secretly sets the order its players fight in. Any teammate can move players up or down. The order is final when every teammate confirms. The other team sees nothing until both teams are final.
  6. The match lobby shows both orders and the team score. The two players who are up press Ready. Everyone else watches each round once it is revealed.
  7. The winner of each 1v1 game stays on and scores 1 point for their team. The loser's team sends its next player, and after the last player it starts again from Player 1.
  8. The first team to reach the wins needed takes the game. Then you can Play again with the same teams.
  9. Leaving a game in progress counts as a loss for your match. A team with nobody left loses the game.

Tips for your first game

  • Start with Load. The score card shows both scores as you : opponent, so keep an eye on which attacks your opponent can use.
  • Each attack has colored dots. They show which defense answers it.
  • In the result card, your move is on the left and your opponent's move is on the right, facing you.
  • Switching to another app can drop your connection. The timer pauses until you are back.
